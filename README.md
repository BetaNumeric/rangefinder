# Rangefinder

A phone compass and distance guessing game. The site is static and can be hosted at a GitHub Pages project URL or at the root of a domain.

This is an app to improve your navigation skills by playing a game. You have to guess how far away a place is from you and in which direction. The goal is to improve your spatial awareness relative to places around you over time.
The game provides you with a compass in the middle and the name of the target place.

To enter your guess, point your phone in the direction you think the place is. Tilt your phone to decrease or increase the value to estimate the distance.

You can tap on your guess to lock it in so you can move freely for your other guess or submit both at once by clicking “submit.”
The next screen shows you the correct answer and rates your distance and direction guess.

Here you can also see the target on a map, the point you guessed, and how far apart they are.

You can choose between several data sets with locations, from local landmarks in Bremen to cities within Germany, capital cities, and places worldwide. In the settings, you can also choose the maximum distance the locations can be from you and toggle dark mode.

The data is saved locally, and you can view your discovered locations on a map. They are colored based on how well you did, and you can click on them to see details.
